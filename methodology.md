============
Identify which sector is trending. Then pick top underlying stocks
once stocks are identified, Check monthly charts and assess the trend. 
1. Check the weekly chart. If stock is oversold then take 3 months future expiry future position
2. Check the daily chart. If stock is oversold then take current month expiry future position

In either case, latest low is the SL trailing loss will be 5%.
